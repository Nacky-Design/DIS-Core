# DIS — Product & Content Strategy · Stage Contract v1
**Stage:** `product_content_strategy` · **Status:** CANDIDATE; author review required before activation.

**Inputs:** actual client baseline, approved Discovery and Competitive Intelligence (when selected), research scope, user scenarios, business objectives, existing texts, product capabilities and evidence statuses.

**Purpose:** derive decision-oriented positioning and a content strategy **within selected Research Scope**. Do not invent verified product capabilities, testimonials, revenue, audience numbers or SEO ranking promises.

**Work:** (1) map Jobs/business outcomes/target segments to site promises, (2) separate accepted business facts from value hypotheses, (3) develop offer alternatives and identify tradeoffs, (4) identify proof required for each claim, (5) define content roles, CTA intent, search/SEO questions and unresolved source requirements, (6) recommend a direction to the operator without treating the recommendation as consent.

**Output:** one versioned strategy result with selected/rejected offer options, evidence trace, content hierarchy, tone rules, UX/IA implications, SEO intent assumptions and validation debt; pin the actual file/decision IDs in client GitHub.

**Stage Acceptance:** primary segment/Jobs and chosen scope identifiable; every business promise supported or explicitly HYPOTHESIS; content/CTA map can feed Semantic Architecture; contradictory messages resolved or flagged; unverified SEO assertions not presented as facts. REQUIRED missing => NEEDS_WORK; quality judgement and final selection remain human.

**Exit:** «Переходим к следующему этапу» triggers Stage Exit Check and summary, not automatic approval. SRA-001 and verified GitHub record precede new SSA.
